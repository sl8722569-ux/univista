Security

Kira handles restricted zones, after-hours activity, loitering, and event priority — and must communicate uncertainty rather than claiming certainty.

  • Secure credential storage, authentication, encryption, access logs, permission checks (later builds).
  • Roles: owner, family, guest, custom — not in Early Access.
  • Evidence package (later): original video, timestamp, camera name, metadata, snapshots, integrity info.
  • AI does not receive unrestricted production access. Human approval before production deploy.
