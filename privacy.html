Privacy

  • Camera, recording, AI events, and spoken replies are off until you enable them.
  • Early Access stores stills in this browser only. Clips download to this device.
  • Cloud, Google Drive, Files, NAS, and sharing are off until a later version with explicit permission.
  • Face processing is optional and off by default. Face blur / plate blur are planned for privacy-safe sharing copies; originals stay separate.
  • Passwords and API keys must never appear in plaintext.
  • Local processing is preferred to reduce unnecessary cloud cost.
