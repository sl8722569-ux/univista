Supported cameras

Every camera gets a status: Fully supported, partially supported, or unsupported. UniVista explains limitations instead of forcing identical behaviour.

FULLY SUPPORTED (EA)

Browser / phone webcam

Live view, snapshot, local clip, motion watch on this device.

SIMULATED

Demo shop / gate

For testing dashboard and grids. Not a physical camera.

PLANNED ADAPTERS

ONVIF, RTSP, Xiaomi, V380, Arlo

Manufacturer adapters will translate into UniVista’s common interface. Status may be partial until a feature is proven.

UNSUPPORTED

No adapter yet

Shown honestly. UniVista learns the camera — the camera does not have to become a UniVista camera.
