Supported lights

Raya (Lighting AI) will control compatible smart/RGB lights: colour, brightness, groups, presets, schedules, and security colours. Not armed in Early Access.

  • Normal = green, Warning = yellow, Security = red, Night = blue (examples).
  • Command example: “Make these lights another colour.”
  • Lights must be compatible and explicitly added by the user.
